Agentic AI for healthcare.

Neodyad is a Canadian research and product company building agentic systems that help healthcare teams work with greater clarity, safety, and focus.

Clinical environments demand reliability and human oversight. We are exploring how autonomous AI agents can support workflows—documentation, coordination, and decision support—while keeping clinicians in control.

We are early. Our work is focused on making advanced AI practical, understandable, and accountable in real care settings.

Principles

  • Human-in-the-loop by design
  • Safety and transparency over speed alone
  • Built for the constraints of clinical work
